THE GLOSS EDIT

Before a lipstick becomes the colour we know and love, it begins with carefully selected pigments. These fine powders are measured and prepared to create the foundation of the final shade.

CHOOSING THE RIGHT PIGMENT: The pigments are combined with oils, waxes, and other ingredients before being gently heated. As the formula melts, solid ingredients transform into a smooth liquid that can be blended together.

CREATING THE PERFECT SHADE: As it's mixed, the shade slowly comes together. What was once separate powder and liquid becomes one smooth, consistent swirl of colour.

TAKING SHAPE: Now the formula is mixed until every ingredient is evenly distributed. The separate colours and textures slowly become one rich, consistent shade with the smooth finish expected from a luxury lipstick.

FINISHED PRODUCT: After the formula has been shaped and finished, the final product is ready for its case and its first swipe. What began as carefully measured pigment has become a finished shade made to be seen, worn, and enjoyed.
